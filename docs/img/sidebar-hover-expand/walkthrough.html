JaBot · issue #209

Hover-to-expand a collapsed sidebar

A closed rail stays a 78px strip. After the toggle is focused or hovered, moving the pointer into the sidebar’s full width slides the list open over the chat. That flyout is not a pin: localStorage, the toggle, and ⌘B still own the remembered open/closed state.

How it works

Collapsed

  • Layout keeps the traffic-lights strip.
  • The list is unmounted, so Tab skips it.
  • A stray pointer over the chat does nothing.
→

Armed

  • Tab to the toggle, or hover it.
  • Focus alone does not mount the list.
  • The hide click arms, but suppresses peek until the pointer leaves the button.
→

Peek

  • Pointer in the 310px rail region slides the list over the chat.
  • open stays false; aria-expanded stays false.
  • Leave the region and the list unmounts again.

Clicking the toggle or pressing Ctrl/⌘B still pins or unpins. Peek never writes jabot.sidebarOpen.

What to look at

  • src/components/Sidebar.tsx — arm on focus/hover; peek from pointer location; suppress after the hide click.
  • src/styles/sidebar.css — collapsed rail is absolutely positioned so the peek overlays instead of shoving the chat.
  • src/styles/tokens.css — --t-sidebar / --ease-sidebar for the width slide.
  • src/App.tsx — unchanged. The shell still owns the pin.

How to verify

  • Hide the sidebar. Tab to “Show sidebar”, then move the mouse into the left ~310px. The list should slide open. Tab order before that hover still skips the list.
  • Click Hide. The list must stay gone until the pointer leaves the toggle and comes back into the rail.
  • Click the toggle or press Ctrl/⌘B. That pins; a reload keeps the choice.
